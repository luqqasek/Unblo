# Unblo presentation notes

Nine slides, three per task. Open `index.html`, use arrows to advance and F for full screen. The PDF contains the same nine slides.

1. **Watermark task.** The illustration is a historical StegaStamp development example, not a prediction from the final submission. White denotes watermarked pixels and black denotes clean pixels. Both classes contribute to mIoU. The earlier model's errors make the small-hole challenge visible.
2. **Final watermark method.** A two-member rank32/family-conditioned2000 DINO recipe supplies image evidence. PixelSeal adds the EMA588 specialist with a feature-CutMix decoder at step200, gain8. Blend 70% ensemble and 30% specialist probabilities, then decode expected mIoU. TrustMark and StegaStamp outputs stay frozen to this recipe. Sources: `task-1/solution/manifest.json`, `model.py`, `reproduce.py`.
3. **Watermark validation.** The organizer accepted submission4129 at public mIoU0.7309. The final PixelSeal local edit scores0.630891 versus0.629340 on57 repeatedly reused development images. Its paired95% interval includes zero and the promotion gate failed. The upload was a requested verification experiment. The public result also changes TrustMark/StegaStamp masks relative to the earlier four-member ensemble, so the gain cannot isolate the final PixelSeal edit. Full replay is for the official test IDs using cached ensemble maps, not a fresh-image service for all families. Sources: `task-1/solution/upload_receipt.json`, `checkpoint-result.json`, `preupload-validation.json`.
4. **Equality task.** Distinguish model identity from serving behavior. Quantization and LoRA change identity under the competition definition, but prompts, sampling and watermarking do not. Source: `task-2/challenge.pdf`.
5. **Equality method.** Explain the v4 decision order and local likelihood measurements. Family assignments and disguise tags include manual analysis. Disclose the ep-26 override informed by earlier public-score feedback. Source: `task-2/research/experiments/s82/scored_v4.py`, `v4.py`.
6. **Equality validation.** The accepted CSV scored 1.0 on the public split. Offline replay reproduces all labels and pair partitions. The saved local test-bed score is lower; known families and development reuse constrain interpretation. Reconstruction deliberately excludes later Qwen-3B likelihood evidence. Sources: `task-2/submission/receipt.json`, `task-2/evidence/predictions.json`, `task-2/replay.py`.
7. **Unlearning task.** We cannot measure gold-model matching or the hidden utility gate with the supplied inputs. Strong/mixed views are preservation proxies, not genuine retain data. Source: `task-3/challenge.pdf`.
8. **Unlearning method.** The hardness rank is mapped through the normal inverse CDF. Targets retain the teacher's relative wrong-class distribution. E50 increases normalized CLS-feature preservation to weight10, retaining the other E30 settings. It uses no external natural anchors or synthetic-bank replacement. Sources: `task-3/src/targets.py`, `task-3/src/unlearn_core.py`, `task-3/evidence/runs-E50_feature10.json`.
9. **Unlearning validation.** E50's public receipt identifies the same SHA-256 as its GPU validation record. These structural checks do not establish true accuracy by themselves. E30 scored0.3013, E43 scored0.3055, and E50 scored0.3149. The observed gain is not a repeatability study, and the final hidden score is unknown.
